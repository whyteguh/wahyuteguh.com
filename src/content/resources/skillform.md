## About this tool

Depth grows the spikes, breadth widens the floor. Skillform redraws your
skill profile as a letterform in real time as you set how deep each field
goes.

- **Your fields** — add the disciplines you work in (up to 16) and drag each one's depth from 0 to 100. Cross the red line at 65 and it becomes a spike.
- **The figure** — a live, animated profile: fields at depth ≥ 65 draw as spikes, fields at ≥ 25 build the floor, and the dimension line measures your breadth.
- **The verdict** — an archetype, a one-line reading of what that shape means, and a formula (`spikes ≥ 65 · fields ≥ 25`).
- **The coach** — spots the field closest to the next threshold and names the exact transition you're one push from (e.g. `T → π`).
- **Taxonomy strip** — load a specimen (I, dash, T, π, M or comb) to see each shape drawn, or roll the dice for a random profile.
- **Quick add** — one-tap chips for common fields, plus a clear-all with a confirm step.

Saved in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
