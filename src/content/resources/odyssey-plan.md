## About this tool

A hands-on take on the Odyssey Plan exercise from *Designing Your Life* — build three versions of your next five years instead of hunting for the one "right" future.

- **Three routes** — chart the Current Path, the Alternative Path (what if this one closes?), and the Wild Path (money and image aside).
- **Route cards** — title, tagline, five yearly milestones, and a short narrative for each route.
- **Gauges** — rate each route on Resources, Likeability, Confidence, and Coherence.
- **Drop anchor** — mark the route that pulls you most, then reflect on what makes you hesitate.

Saved in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
