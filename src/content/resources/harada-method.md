## About this tool

The 9×9 goal grid from the Harada Method — the technique Noboru Harada developed and Shohei Ohtani used to map out his baseball dream. A 3×3 master grid sits around one core ambition, and each surrounding cell expands into its own 3×3 sub-grid of micro-actions.

- **Core ambition** — your single most important goal sits in the dead center of the master grid.
- **Eight pillars** — the surrounding cells; type a pillar here and it auto-fills the center of its sub-grid everywhere.
- **64 micro-actions** — eight small, tactical tasks per pillar, each with its own completion toggle.
- **Macro view** — see the whole 9×9 map at a glance, with a color-coded overview per pillar (blue = has content, green = done).
- **Focus view** — zoom into one pillar for a mobile-friendly, color-coded editing board.
- **Live progress** — per-pillar percentage and an overall completion score, updated as you tick tasks.
- **Export / Import** — download your grid as JSON and restore it anywhere.
- **Print / PDF** — a clean, print-ready layout of the full grid.

Saved in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
