## About this tool

A habit garden that's honest about failure.

- **Plan the loop** — build each habit as *after what → I will → payoff*, with the sentence writing itself as you type.
- **Streaks that grow** — your plant sprouts leaves and blooms as the streak climbs, and flatlines when you miss two days.
- **Fallback saves** — miss a day? Do the smallest possible version instead; define one on the spot if you never set it.
- **The cemetery** — when a habit really dies, bury it with an honest cause and a note, and learn which killers keep coming back.
- **Resurrect** — dig one up and plant it again from scratch.

Saved in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
