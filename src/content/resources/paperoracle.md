## About this tool

A four-colour reading of the humans around you, after the DISC-style model
popularized by *Surrounded by Idiots*. Describe the person in question, then
cut and reveal the verdict.

- **Nine sliders** — talks, decides, notices detail, patience, impulsiveness, need for control, shows emotion, loves people, standards.
- **The paper person** — a jointed little figure that changes colour, posture, accessories and mood as you move the sliders: glasses for detail, a tie for control, a clipboard for standards, a flower for patience, hearts for sociability, and more.
- **The dress logic** — the shirt is cut from the loudest ink and brightened so it stands out; the trousers borrow the runner-up colour when the mix is close; the tiny pocket square shows the exact blend.
- **Presets & shuffle** — load the Boss, the Analyst, the Entertainer or the Peacemaker, or shuffle a random stranger.
- **The verdict** — cut the paper with the scissors animation and a stamped reading appears: the colour mix, how to spot them, their superpowers, how to handle them with care, their secret fear, and how to survive them.
- **Copy the verdict** — take the reading with you as text.

Saved in your browser only — the whole trick runs locally, nothing is sent anywhere.
