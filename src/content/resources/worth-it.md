## About this tool

The smallest sacrifice for the biggest payoff. Before you buy the thing or take
on the commitment, put it on the scale.

- **The costs** — price, any ongoing monthly cost, the hours of your life it takes to earn, and the energy or effort it drains.
- **The benefits** — how often you'll use or feel it, how long it lasts, the joy it brings, and its usefulness.
- **Worth It Score** — a 0–100 reading, with a verdict band from "Worth it." to "Skip it."
- **The math** — total cost (including the value of your time and an energy-friction penalty), estimated uses, and the real cost per use.
- **The verdict** — plain-language reasoning about what's dragging the score, plus lighter alternatives (wait 30 days, rent first, kill the recurring cost, buy the 80% version).
- **Scenarios** — load "a new phone", "gym membership", "impulse splurge" and more to see how the score moves.

Saved in your browser only — nothing is sent anywhere.
