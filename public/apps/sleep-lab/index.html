sleep science, in small tools

Sleep Lab

A handful of calculators built on consensus sleep research — how much sleep you need, when to go to bed, how much debt you're carrying, and how to plan a nap or a polyphasic schedule. Everything runs locally.

where these numbers come from

Recommended hours by age follow the joint consensus of the American Academy of Sleep Medicine, Sleep Research Society, and American Academy of Pediatrics. Sleep-cycle timing (~90 min per cycle, 10–20% deep, 20–25% REM), the 10-3-2-1-0 wind-down rule, and nap guidance (≤20 min or ~90 min) come from the Sleep Foundation's medical review. These tools are educational, not medical advice — talk to a clinician about persistent problems.
